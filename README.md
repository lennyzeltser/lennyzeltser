My name is Lenny Zeltser. I'm a cybersecurity executive with deep technical roots, product management experience, and a business mindset. I've built security products and programs from early stage to enterprise scale. I'm also a Faculty Fellow at SANS Institute and the creator of REMnux, a popular Linux toolkit for malware analysis. I share my perspectives on security leadership and technology at [zeltser.com](https://zeltser.com).

## Projects in Other Organizations

[REMnux](https://remnux.org) is a free Linux toolkit for reverse-engineering and analyzing malicious software. I created it and maintain it with the community, and I keep the code in the [REMnux organization](https://github.com/REMnux). That includes the [distribution](https://github.com/REMnux/distro), the [Docker images](https://github.com/REMnux/docker), and an [MCP server](https://github.com/REMnux/remnux-mcp-server) that exposes the toolkit to AI assistants.

[AI Defense Matrix](https://aidefensematrix.com) is a framework for defending AI systems that I created with Sounil Yu. It aligns with NIST CSF 2.0 and extends the [Cyber Defense Matrix](https://cyberdefensematrix.com). The [AI Defense Matrix Catalog](https://catalog.aidefensematrix.com) maps products that secure AI to the framework, with [catalog data](https://github.com/aidefensematrix/aidefensematrix-catalog-data) open for contributions.
